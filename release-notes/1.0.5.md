## What's new in 1.0.5

### New

- A progress bar under the update download and under VOICE downloads.
- FOCUS, next to the pin in Settings (off by default): when on, clicking
  Settings makes it the active window.

### Fixed

- Voices work: 1.0.4 was built without its voice engines, so no engine
  loaded. Piper, Kokoro, the Microsoft online voices and the Windows voices
  all speak again.
- The refinery work-order tracker was also missing from 1.0.4 and is back.
- Drop-down lists in Settings (VOICE engine, voice, what it says, monitor) no
  longer vanish after a second or two.
