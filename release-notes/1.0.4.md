## What's new in 1.0.4

### New

- A VOICE group in Settings. Pick the speech engine, the voice, the speed and
  what is said ("Borase, times 3", "Three Borase", or with the signature
  number), then press TEST to hear it. Four engines, side by side:
  - Windows voices: built in and offline (the default).
  - Piper: offline and natural. An optional add-on (GPL-3.0), downloaded
    only when you choose it.
  - Kokoro: offline and the most natural, but uses more CPU. An optional
    add-on (GPL-3.0), downloaded only when you choose it.
  - Microsoft online voices: online. Each phrase is sent to Microsoft.

  If an engine fails, a Windows voice speaks instead. Add-ons are stored in
  `%LOCALAPPDATA%\SC Assistant\voices`.
- A DEBUG group in Settings, all off by default. SCAN HISTORY keeps the last
  50 reads in a text file; SAVE BAD READS keeps pictures of plates that
  could not be read.
- Logs: the app now writes warnings and errors to `app.log`. DETAILED LOG
  adds more detail, VIEW LOG shows the log live in a window, and EXPORT
  DIAGNOSTICS saves a zip on your Desktop to attach to a bug report (never
  your notes, inventory or blueprints).

### Changed

- The voice settings moved from LOOK & SOUND to the new VOICE group.

### Fixed

- Very large scans are no longer dropped: the scanner now reads numbers up to
  120,000 (it stopped at 100,000), on every install, with no reset needed.
  A number that fits no single kind, such as the 112,000 seen over Aphorite
  near Cellin, is shown with a yellow border and saved under Unmatched
  readings so it can be looked at.
- SHOW PREVIEW: the green box now sits on the number itself instead of
  slightly beside it.
